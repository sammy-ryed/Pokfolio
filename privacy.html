Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 5, 2026

Pokfolio ("we", "our", "the app") generates a personalized stat card based on data from a user's own GitHub and/or Pinterest account. This page explains what data we access, how it's used, and how it's handled.

What data we collect

When you connect your GitHub or Pinterest account, we access publicly available profile information and account statistics, such as:

  • Your username, display name, and profile photo
  • Public activity counts (e.g. repositories, stars, followers, pins, boards)

For Pinterest specifically, we only ever access the data of the Pinterest account that has authenticated and authorized our app (yours). We do not access other users' Pinterest data.

How we use your data

Your data is used solely to generate your stat card. We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond generating and displaying this card to you.

What we don't do

  • We do not sell your data to any third party.
  • We do not share your data with advertisers.
  • We do not post, pin, or modify anything on your connected accounts.

Data storage

We store the minimum data needed to generate and display your card (such as your username and the stats used on the card). Authentication tokens are stored securely and are only used to make the specific API calls needed to generate your card.

Revoking access

You can disconnect Pokfolio's access to your Pinterest account at any time from your Pinterest account settings . You can revoke GitHub access from your GitHub authorized applications settings .

Contact

If you have questions about this policy or want your data removed, open an issue on our GitHub repository .

This policy may be updated as the app develops. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
